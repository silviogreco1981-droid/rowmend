PRIVACY BY ARCHITECTURE

Your spreadsheet is not our dataset.

RowMend is designed as a local-first tool. Parsing, validation and SQL generation happen in the browser. This MVP has no application backend and no account system.

That makes it suitable for evaluating the workflow before adding any cloud features later.
