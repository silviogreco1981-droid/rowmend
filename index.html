WHY ROWMEND

Not another one-off CSV checker.

RowMend keeps the useful parts of a data workflow together: cleanup rules, contracts, validation mappings, output settings and privacy-minimized run history.

Reusable Local Projects keep configuration together. Comparable baselines show what changed on the next delivery. Actionable outputs include clean/error CSV and safe SQL generation. Migration-aware source/target reconciliation is available when needed.
